Programme Overview

The goal of this FDP is to bring together renowned experts conducting pioneering research in the field of Quantum Computing to disseminate knowledge among faculty members, research scholars, and postgraduate students who wish to explore this emerging technological frontier.

The professionals will share their expertise and experience to inspire participants to pursue research and innovation in Quantum Computing and its applications.

Who can attend: Faculty members from colleges, universities, and professional institutes, as well as students, researchers, and industry personnel working in related disciplines.

Other eminent personnel from IITs, IIITs, NITs, and industry experts shall deliver lectures and hands-on sessions as part of the FDP.

Hands-On Sessions

  • Introduction to QML and hybrid quantum–classical models using quantum classifiers such as Qiskit or PennyLane.
  • Building qubits with trapped ions and exploring Linear Optics-based Quantum Computation using Strawberry Fields (Xanadu) or a similar simulators.
  • Studying bit-flip and phase-flip errors using a 3-qubit repetition code in Qiskit.
  • Implementing Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) through the BB84 protocol through simulation tools such as Qiskit or QuNetSim.
  • Simulating noisy channels to demonstrate how quantum error correction improves fidelity.
