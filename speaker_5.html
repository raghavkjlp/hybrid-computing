Dr. Amit D. Lad

Affiliation: IIT Mandi

Abstract: Dr. Amit D. Lad’s research focuses on quantum information processing, quantum computation, and quantum optics. He explores the design and analysis of quantum systems for computation, communication, and metrology. With numerous publications in leading journals, Dr. Lad contributes to advancing experimental and theoretical aspects of quantum technologies. He is currently affiliated with IIT Mandi, where he mentors students and leads research in quantum technologies.
