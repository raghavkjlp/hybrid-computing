Electronics & ICT Academy — PDPM IIITDM Jabalpur

The Electronics and ICT Academy at PDPM IIITDM Jabalpur, established under the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, focuses on building capacity through advanced training in electronics and ICT.

It offers specialized programmes, internships, research, and consultancy in emerging areas, enabling the development of technical skills and research excellence across academia and industry.

Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala

Established in 1956, TIET Patiala stands as a premier institution in engineering education, research, and innovation. With a sprawling 250-acre campus, it offers world-class facilities and a vibrant academic ecosystem.

The institute nurtures a culture of innovation and multidisciplinary collaboration, empowering young minds to contribute to national and global advancements in technology and research.
