Prof.Arzoo Miglani

Affiliation:TIET Patiala

Abstract: Arzoo Miglani received the B.Tech. degree from GJU, Hisar, in 2009, and the M.E. and Ph.D. degrees in information security from the Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET), Patiala, in 2015 and 2024, respectively. She served as an Assistant Professor at Chitkara University for one year, at DIT University, Dehradun, for two years, and at Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET) for one year. She is currently working as assistant professor with the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, Patiala, Punjab, India. She is GATE-qualified. Her research interests include wireless sensor networks, network security, blockchain, and content-centric networking
